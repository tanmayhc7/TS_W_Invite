# Product

## Purpose
A personal save-the-date for the wedding of Shalome & Tanmay. It tells friends the date (March 5, 2027), the place (Goa), and that a formal invitation will follow.

## Users
Friends of the couple, opening a shared link, most likely on a phone. They are not evaluating a product; they are receiving good news and should enjoy opening it.

## Experience (confirmed, keep)
Swipe right to begin, then a short animation, then scratch a cloud to reveal the date, then the invitation card. The four screens, the copy, and this order stay.

## Voice
The owner's words: "just like an invitation for a celebration." Warm and celebratory, not corporate and not heavy.

## Constraints
- Keep the scenery exactly as painted: the trees, the house, the couple, the blue sky, the sea. More detail may be added on top, but nothing in the scene is removed or replaced.
- Do not hide the painted birds.
- Keep existing copy. New wording needs the owner's approval.
- Mobile first. Must work with touch, mouse, and keyboard, and respect reduced-motion settings.

## Platform
web (mobile web)

## Stack
Plain static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages. No build step.
